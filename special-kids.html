How we teach command following

One short command, one clear demo, every single time — so each child knows exactly what comes next.
1Listen
Coach gives one short, calm command — no long instructions.
2Look
Coach shows the move slowly — a visual demo every time.
3Do
Child copies the move at their own pace, with gentle help.
4Celebrate
Instant praise, a high-five or a sticker — every success counts.

What research says

Autism
Repetitive behaviours fell by about 42%
30 children with autism did 14 weeks of kata training. Stereotyped behaviours dropped and stayed lower one month later.
Bahrami et al., 2012 · Research in Developmental Disabilities
Autism
Better self-control & working memory
43 children aged 8–11 with autism trained in martial arts for 13 weeks. Parents also reported better emotion and behaviour control.
Phung & Goldberg · Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders
ADHD
Sharper selective attention
Teenagers with ADHD who trained Taekwondo twice a week scored better on attention tests than a group that didn't.
Kadri et al., 2019 · Int. J. Environ. Res. Public Health
All children
Better attention, fewer behaviour problems
240 children aged 7–11 took an 11-week Taekwondo course. Teachers saw better attention control and fewer conduct problems.
Ng-Knight et al., 2022 · Developmental Psychology
Martial arts supports therapy — it doesn't replace it. We're happy to work alongside your child's doctor, therapist or school.
